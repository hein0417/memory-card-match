#  Tips & Tricks — Memory Card Match

Want to finish the game faster and with fewer moves? Here are some useful tips to help you win!

---

##  Basic Rules (Quick Reminder)

- Flip 2 cards per turn
- If they match → they stay face up 
- If they don't match → they flip back over 
- Goal: find all pairs with the fewest moves and fastest time

---

##  Tips To Win

### 1.  Watch Every Card Carefully
Even when it's not your turn to match, **pay attention to every card that flips**.
The more you remember, the fewer moves you waste later.

### 2.  Start From One Corner
Don't click cards randomly all over the board.
Start from one corner and **move in order** — left to right, row by row.
This way you build a mental map of where each card is.

### 3.  Remember Wrong Flips
When two cards don't match, **don't forget them!**
Those cards will come back later. If you remember what they were,
you can match them as soon as you find their pair.

### 4.  Go Slow at the Start
At the beginning of the game, **flip cards slowly and think** before clicking.
Rushing early means you forget positions and waste moves later.

### 5.  Match What You Already Know First
If you already remember where a pair is — **match it immediately!**
Don't flip an unknown card first. Go for the guaranteed match.

### 6. Use the Move Counter as Your Score
Try to beat your own record each game.
If you finished Easy with 20 moves last time, try to do it in 15 next time.

### 7.  Play on a Bigger Screen
If you can, play on a computer screen instead of a small phone.
Seeing all cards at once makes it easier to remember positions.

##  Difficulty Guide

| Level | Grid | Pairs | Best Strategy |
|-------|------|-------|---------------|
| Easy | 4×4 | 8 pairs | Good for warm-up and beginners |
| Medium | 5×4 | 10 pairs | Requires more focus |
| Hard | 6×5 | 15 pairs | Challenge mode — go slow! |

---

##  Memory Techniques

### The Grouping Method
Mentally divide the board into sections (top, middle, bottom).
Focus on memorizing one section at a time instead of the whole board.

### The Repeat Method
When you flip a card, **say the emoji out loud in your head**.
For example: *"fox — top left corner"*
Saying it helps your brain remember the position better.

### The Patience Method
After flipping a non-matching pair, **pause for 1 second** before your next move.
That short pause helps your brain lock in the positions.

---

##  Challenge Yourself

Once you feel comfortable, try these personal challenges:

-  Finish Easy mode in **under 10 moves**
-  Finish Medium mode in **under 30 seconds**
-  Finish Hard mode without **any wrong matches**
-  Complete all 3 difficulties in a row without taking a break

---

##  Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Don't click too fast** — you won't remember what you saw
- **Don't ignore unmatched cards** — they are clues for later
- **Don't give up on Hard mode** — it just takes more practice!
